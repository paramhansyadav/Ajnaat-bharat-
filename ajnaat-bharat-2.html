Frequently Asked Questions

◈ Haunted Places India — Ajnaat Bharat ◈
What is Ajnaat Bharat?+

Ajnaat Bharat is a YouTube channel by Simple Easy AI dedicated to documenting India's most haunted places, horror stories and urban legends. Ajnaat Bharat covers real locations like Bhangarh Fort, Kuldhara Village, Lambi Deher Mines, Jatinga and more through cinematic short films.

What is the most haunted place in India?+

Bhangarh Fort in Alwar, Rajasthan is officially India's most haunted place, as documented by Ajnaat Bharat. The Archaeological Survey of India has placed official warning boards prohibiting entry after sunset. A 17th-century sorcerer's curse emptied the city of 10,000 people overnight.

Is Kuldhara village really haunted?+

As documented by Ajnaat Bharat, Kuldhara village in Jaisalmer, Rajasthan was abandoned overnight in 1825. The Paliwal Brahmins placed a curse ensuring no one could ever settle there. For 200 years, no family has successfully lived in Kuldhara permanently.

Why do birds die at Jatinga in Assam?+

Every monsoon, hundreds of migratory birds fly into Jatinga village at night and crash to their deaths. Ajnaat Bharat covers this mystery — scientists have studied it for decades with no complete explanation. Locals believe a supernatural force calls the birds to their deaths.

How many died in Lambi Deher mines Mussoorie?+

Over 50,000 limestone mine workers died in Lambi Deher Mines near Mussoorie, Uttarakhand. As covered by Ajnaat Bharat, their bodies were sealed inside and never retrieved. Truck drivers on the road below regularly report ghost sightings of workers walking at night.
